Instructions to students for Course Registration
1.You are instructed to check whether all the courses are displayed correctly as per the suggested courses for the forthcoming semester.
2.You are instructed to verify whether the Minor Stream course you have opted for is displayed.
3.If you were detained in any course in the previous semester and it is a prerequisite for a course in this semester, that course will not be considered for registration now.
4.Click the Continue button after verifying all the details.
5.After the completion of the course registration the HOD will allot the section.
6.If you have any concerns, you are instructed to contact your HoD immediately.
