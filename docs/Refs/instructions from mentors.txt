*CodeForge BuildX 2026 – Project Documentation Guidelines*

Dear Students,

All mini-project documentation must follow a *uniform professional format*.

📄 *Formatting Requirements:*
- *Paper:* A4, Portrait
- *Font:* Times New Roman
- *Body Text:* 12 pt
- *Main Headings:* 16 pt, Bold
- *Subheadings:* 14 pt, Bold
- *Line Spacing:* 1.5
- *Text Alignment:* Justified
- *Margins:* 1 inch on all sides
- *Paragraph Spacing:* 6 pt after each paragraph
- *Page Numbers:* Mandatory (bottom center/right)
- *Text Color:* Black
- *Background:* Plain White
- *Length:* 10–15 pages (including cover page)

📌 *Additional Guidelines:*
1. Follow the *six mandatory documentation sections* already shared in the BuildX guidelines.
2. Use consistent heading numbering (e.g., 1, 1.1, 1.2).
3. *Images/Figures:* Captions must be numbered and placed *below the image* (e.g., Figure 1: Login Page).
4. *Tables:* Captions must be numbered and placed *above the table* (e.g., Table 1: Technology Stack).
5. Include clear, properly cropped screenshots without stretching or distortion.
6. Avoid excessive blank spaces, unnecessary decorations, repeated content, or copying entire source code files.
7. Use clear English with proper spelling and grammar.

📌 *Submission:* Submit the documentation as a *PDF* and bring a *printed hard copy* for mentor review.

⚠️ *Documentation that does not follow these guidelines must be corrected before final approval.*
